Fit calculator

    Calibration from a test print

    Print a few holes and pins of known CAD size, measure them with calipers and enter one pair per line: CAD size, measured size.

    Tolerance test coupon (STL)

    A plate with a row of holes (or pegs) stepping the clearance. Each step is marked by bumps: a bar counts 5, a dot counts 1. Sizes are raw CAD sizes, not compensated.

    Metric screw cheat sheet

    Typical values only — starting points to test. Heat-set insert holes vary by insert brand: check the datasheet.

    Self-tap, clearance and insert holes are CAD sizes; add your hole compensation if needed. Nut trap sizes already include the profile's hole compensation plus a small clearance.
